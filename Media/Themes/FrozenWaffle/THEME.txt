Frozen Waffle theme
====================

Premium frozen variants of the Burnt Waffle icon set — same compositions
and waffle-grid motifs, re-themed with ice, frost, and cold cyan glow.
Warm accent colors (golden waffle tan, bronze, amber, quest yellow) add
depth and layering against the icy palette (see Housing as reference).

Source art: AI-generated from Burnt Waffle references (1536×1024), then
post-processed via tools/ProcessFrozenIcons.cs:
  - Scale-to-fit within 256×256 at 90% (preserves full composition)
  - Key near-pure-black background to transparent (keeps dark ice shadows)

Files: Collections.png … GameMenu.png (standard micro menu names)

Raw sources: source/FrozenWaffle_{Name}.png
Production icons: this folder
