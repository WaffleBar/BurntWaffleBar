The BDK theme
=============

Retail WoW Blood Death Knight (Midnight) — deep crimson blood, ivory bone,
blackened saronite, San'layn vampire glow, and Deathbringer reaper shadow.
Spec-flavored sibling of The Death Knight; pick it manually so Blood tanks get
Bone Shield / Dancing Rune Weapon fantasy without replacing the default DK pack.

NOT a frost/unholy recolor and NOT a Ret/Fire Mage clone. Motifs are deliberately
Blood-only:
  Bone Shield crest, crossed runeblades, bone-bound tome, Acherus blood citadel,
  Dancing Rune Weapons, blood parchment scroll, skull blood trophy, Runeforge,
  Reaper's Mark talent sigil, Blood DK portrait, Deathbringer/San'layn guild,
  twin blood chalices, bone rune menu plate — no frost ice, no teal water,
  no holy gold fist.

Shape language: freeform function silhouettes (outer cutout follows the object).

Source: bespoke multi-material AI art. Processed via tools/ProcessPreserveThemeIcons.cs
(ProcessTheBDK.cs runner).

  Raw:  source/TheBDK_{Name}.png
  Out:  this folder / {Name}.png

Select in /bw → Icon Theme → The BDK
(turn off Use Class Theme if you are a Death Knight on the default The Death Knight pack).
