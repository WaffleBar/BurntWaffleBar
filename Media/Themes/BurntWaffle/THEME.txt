Burnt Waffle Icon Theme
=======================

Premium hand-styled icons: charred waffle-grid textures with molten orange
glow, layered warm depth (char black, burnt umber, golden amber, hot
yellow cores), ember sparks, and unique molten syrup drips per icon.

Source art: AI-generated from legacy Burnt Waffle references (1536×1024),
post-processed via tools/ProcessBurntIcons.cs:
  - Scale-to-fit within 256×256 at 90%
  - Key near-pure-black background to transparent

Raw sources: source/BurntWaffle_{Name}.png
Production icons: this folder ({Name}.png)

File names (must match exactly):
  Collections.png         Warband Collections
  PVP.png                 Player vs. Player
  AdventureGuide.png      Adventure Guide
  Housing.png             Housing
  GroupFinder.png         Group Finder
  QuestTracker.png        Quest Tracker
  AchievementTracker.png  Achievement Tracker
  Talents.png             Talents & Spellbook
  Character.png           Character (replaces your portrait icon)
  Guild.png               Guild
  Social.png              Social
  GameMenu.png            Game Menu

Select the active theme in /bw under Icon Theme.
