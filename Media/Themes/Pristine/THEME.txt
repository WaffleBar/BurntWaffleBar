Pristine theme
================

Clear glass icons: translucent centers, thick opaque white rims, broad top-left gleam.
Supersampled edges. Reference-bar silhouettes with high-fidelity extraction.

All effects baked into each PNG. No runtime layering.

Regenerate:
  tools/ReferenceIconExtract.cs

PowerShell:
  $ref = "...\Media\Themes\Pristine\reference-bar.png"
  $out = "...\Media\Themes\Pristine"
  $cs = Get-Content "...\tools\ReferenceIconExtract.cs" -Raw
  Add-Type -TypeDefinition $cs -ReferencedAssemblies System.Drawing -IgnoreWarnings
  [ReferenceIconExtract]::ExtractAll($ref, $out)
