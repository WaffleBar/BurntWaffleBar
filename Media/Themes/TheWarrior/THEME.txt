The Warrior theme
=================

Retail WoW Warrior class identity — Iron and honor — battered plate, crimson war paint, Alliance steel and Horde fury.

Procedurally drawn micro-menu icons with unique Warrior
class props (weapons, emblems, armor) per button.

Pipeline: tools/DrawClassThemeSources.cs + tools/ClassThemeBuilder.cs
  Raw:  source/TheWarrior_{Name}.png
  Out:  this folder / {Name}.png

Select in /bw → Icon Theme → The Warrior.
