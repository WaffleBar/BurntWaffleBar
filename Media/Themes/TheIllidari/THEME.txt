The Illidari theme
====================

Modern retail WoW Demon Hunter / Illidari identity — fel-green infernal
power, obsidian leather, demonic runes, and the official class-purple UI
accent (#A330C9) on hover and clock rim. Outlawed fel-weapon fantasy;
NO waffle grid or breakfast texture.

Visual pillars (Legion / Dragonflight / The War Within era):
  - Fel flame highlights (#55FF88 → #B8FFB0 inner burn)
  - Obsidian / charcoal leather midtones
  - Deep violet-black shadows (Vault of the Wardens, Black Temple)
  - Class-purple gleam on brightest rim pixels (raid-frame recognition)
  - Warglaives, blindfold, demonic sigils, fel crystals — Illidan's legacy

Lore touchstones:
  - The Illidari: demon hunters who bind fel to destroy the Legion
  - Mardum, Black Temple, Vault of the Wardens, Fel Rush / Metamorphosis
  - Spectral sight, tattooed runes, consumed demon hearts

Source art: AI-generated Illidari micro-menu icons (square, black background).

Pipeline:
  tools/ProcessTheIllidariIcons.cs
    Raw:  source/TheIllidari_{Name}.png
    Out:  this folder / {Name}.png
    - 512px scale/crop → fel contact shadow → 256px downscale
    - Preserves source colors (gold fel, purple rim) — no SDF recolor

Files: Collections.png … GameMenu.png (standard micro menu names)

Select in /bw → Icon Theme → The Illidari.
